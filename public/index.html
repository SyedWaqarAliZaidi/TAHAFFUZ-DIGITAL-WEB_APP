Know your rights

General guidance, not legal advice.

Harassment is not your fault

Street and workplace harassment can be reported. You do not need to be sure it is a crime to call 15.

Before you travel

Share your trip, note the vehicle plate, keep your phone charged, and tell a guardian your route.

If you feel followed

Move to a busy, lit place, call a guardian, and use Call 15. Do not confront the person.
